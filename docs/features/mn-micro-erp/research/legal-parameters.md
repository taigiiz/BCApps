# Mongolian legal parameters: canonical table for `tax_parameter`

- **Date of re-verification:** 2026-10-06. This table replaces the "(fact-check: unverified)" values in [`mn-tax.md`](mn-tax.md) and [`mn-accounting.md`](mn-accounting.md) as the seed for the effective-dated parameter table.
- **Seed file:** [`../db/seed/legal_parameters.sql`](../db/seed/legal_parameters.sql). It is generated from the same data as the table below, so the two always match: 115 rows, 101 distinct `param_code`s (5 rows added on 2026-10-08 by db/CHANGE_REQUESTS.md: `cit.simplified_return_rate` and four PosAPI 3.0 technical limits `ebarimt.*`). It loads on PostgreSQL 16 with `ON_ERROR_STOP=1`, can be re-run safely, and no `param_code` has overlapping effective periods. All three properties were checked on 2026-10-06.
- **Method and limits.** This pass ran about 60 targeted web searches in English and Mongolian. Direct page fetches were blocked by the egress proxy for pwc.com, legalinfo.mn, mof.gov.mn, gogo.mn, d.parliament.mn and lawforum.parliament.mn, and mains.mn did not resolve. Every value therefore comes from search-engine extracts of the cited pages, not from a full reading of the consolidated law text.
  - One extra secondary source was used: the open-source **OpenAccountants** Mongolia guides (GitHub `ryanduguid/openaccountants`, commit `44d2f8d`, 2026-10-04). They cite legalinfo.mn article numbers but are marked "AI-drafted, pending local-accountant review". They count as **one** secondary source and never verify a value on their own.
  - Before go-live, a Mongolian tax adviser should sign off every row against legalinfo.mn.

## How to read the table

| Field | Convention |
|---|---|
| `status` | **verified**: in this pass, two or more independent sources agree on both the value and the effective date, or one official primary source (legalinfo.mn, MTA, MoF or the eBarimt PosAPI reference) confirms it. **unverified**: only one source was found, the sources conflict, or the effective date is unclear. |
| `confidence` | **high**: several sources agree and the value is long-standing. **medium**: credible but thin evidence, or a missing detail. **low**: weak or old extracts, or a conflict. Values that could not be checked at all stay `unverified` with `low` confidence. |
| `value` / `unit` | Rates use `ratio` as decimal fractions (0.10 = 10%). Amounts are in `MNT`. `day_of_following_month` and `day_of_month_after_quarter` hold a day number. `month_day_following_year` holds a `MM-DD` text in `value_text`. A `flag` is 1 or 0. `fine_units` are converted with `fine.unit_value_mnt`. `json` holds a structured table in `value_text`. |
| `effective_from` | The law's start date when it is known (VAT Law 2016-01-01; CIT, PIT and General Tax Law 2020-01-01; progressive PIT 2023-01-01). Otherwise it is the earliest date this research confirms the value was in force (for example 2024-01-01 for the social-insurance rates). **The table is not a complete history before that date.** |
| `effective_to` | NULL means open-ended. A value replaced by the 2026 reform ends the day before its successor starts. |
| Lookup rule | Pick the row where `param_code` matches and `effective_from <= d <= coalesce(effective_to, 'infinity')`. Use the posting date as `d`, or the tax-period start date for period taxes. Rows marked `unverified` may feed warnings, defaults and reports, but the specs should not let them drive automatic postings without an override recorded in the audit trail. |

## Resolved questions (a)–(k)

**(a) Start date of the MNT 400 M VAT registration threshold: 2027-07-01.** Most of the tax package starts on 2027-01-01, but the threshold clause starts on **2027-07-01**.
- Sources for the date: Mongolian press on the final vote ([gogo.mn](https://gogo.mn/r/v1656y), [ikon.mn](https://ikon.mn/n/3ocs), where 72 MPs voted to keep the clause) and the English-language UB Post of 2026-06-22 ([PressReader](https://www.pressreader.com/mongolia/the-ub-post/20260622/281509347896954)).
- No English Big-4 extract found in this pass gives a different date for this clause. They only state 2027-01-01 for the package as a whole.
- Consumer VAT-refund tiers also start on 2027-07-01 ([parliament.mn](https://www.parliament.mn/nn/76672/)), so the VAT Law amendment has its own later start date.
- Seeded as: MNT 50 M to 2027-06-30, then MNT 400 M from 2027-07-01. Status verified, confidence medium, because the law text itself was not read.

**(b) Simplified VAT regime: confirmed in outline, details open.**
- Confirmed: VAT payers with turnover below MNT 400 M count **90% of quarterly sales as deemed purchases**, so VAT payable is about 1% of sales, and they file and pay **quarterly** ([vatcalc](https://www.vatcalc.com/mongolia/mongolia-vat-reforms/), [vatupdate 2026-07-09](https://www.vatupdate.com/2026/07/09/mongolia-uses-vat-relief-as-cornerstone-of-tax-reform-agenda/), [KPMG July 2026](https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmgsites/mn/pdf/Mongolian-Tax-Law-Update-2026.pdf.coredownload.inline.pdf)).
- Still open:
  - the start date: 2027-01-01 (package) or 2027-07-01 (threshold)
  - whether taxpayers opt in or are placed in the regime automatically
  - whether the base is VAT-exclusive
  - the quarterly due day
  - the excluded activities (mining, petroleum, alcohol, tobacco and fuel trade come from one extract)
- All simplified-regime rows are `unverified`.

**(c) CIT small-business reliefs, now vs 2027.**
- **Now:**
  - The 1% rate applies to annual revenue of **MNT 300 M** or less (CIT Law art. 20.2.7).
  - The **90% CIT credit** applies to revenue of **MNT 1.5 bn** or less.
  - The simplified annual return TT-02(ХГ) at 1% of revenue applies to non-VAT-payers with revenue below **MNT 50 M**.
  - Mining, petroleum, alcohol and tobacco are excluded from all three.
- **From 2027-01-01:**
  - The 90% credit ceiling rises to **MNT 2.5 bn** (verified, high).
  - The simplified 1% regime ceiling rises to **MNT 400 M** (UB Post and KPMG summaries; `unverified`, because it is unclear whether it replaces the 300 M 1% rate or only the 50 M simplified return, and whether it starts on 2027-07-01).
- Still open: the base of the 1% rate (revenue or taxable income) and whether the 300 M group files semi-annually (Mongolian press) or annually (PwC).

**(d) CIT and VAT filing deadlines, now vs 2027.**
- **Now:**
  - VAT: monthly, filed and paid by the **10th** of the next month (VAT Law art. 16.1; verified, high).
  - CIT: quarterly returns by the **20th** of the month after the quarter.
  - Annual CIT and withholding returns: by **10 February** (verified, high).
- **2027:**
  - KPMG's July 2026 newsletter moves the annual filing and payment deadline to **5 March**.
  - A reform summary moves the **quarterly payment** to the **last day of the month after the quarter**.
  - Both rows are `unverified`: each has a single source, and it is unclear whether FY2026 returns due in 2027 already use 5 March.
  - No change to the monthly VAT day was found.
  - Returns can be amended for **2 years** from 2027 (verified).

**(e) Social insurance (НДШ).**
- **Employee:** 11.5% in both 2026 and 2027. It is made up of pension 8.5%, benefits 0.8%, unemployment 0.2% and health 2%.
- **Employer, 2026:** pension 8.5%, benefits 1.0%, unemployment 0.5% and accident 0.5, 1.5 or 2.5% by risk class. That gives subtotals of **10.5, 11.5 or 12.5%**, plus 2% health.
- **Employer, from 2027-01-01** (Social Insurance General Law amendment of 2026-07-02):
  - Subtotals fall to **10.4, 11.3 or 12.3%**, plus 2% health ([ikon.mn/n/3qs6](https://ikon.mn/n/3qs6); verified, high).
  - The component split is accident 0.3, 1.2 or 2.2% and unemployment 0.6%. MLSP confirms only the direction (accident down, unemployment up), so the split is `unverified`, medium.
- **Insurable ceiling:** 10 × the minimum wage. The employee contribution cap was MNT 910,800 a month from 2025-04-01 ([PwC alert 01/2025](https://www.pwc.com/mn/en/tax_alerts/tax_alert_01_2025.html)). With a minimum wage of MNT 1,000,000 the 2027 cap would be 10,000,000 × 11.5% = MNT 1,150,000. That figure is computed, not seeded.

**(f) PIT bands.**
- **2026:** 10% up to MNT 10 M a month (120 M a year), 15% up to 15 M (180 M), and 20% above (verified, high).
- **2027-01-01:** a **0% band** for employment income up to the minimum wage (verified for the rate).
- **2028-01-01:** a **1% band** from the end of the 0% band up to MNT 2,000,000 a month (verified, medium).
- **Conflict on the 0% band ceiling:**
  - Amendment summaries say "0–792,000", which was the minimum wage when the law passed ([ikon 3ok6](https://ikon.mn/n/3ok6), [mnb.mn](https://www.mnb.mn/i/349937)).
  - [ikon.mn/n/3po4](https://ikon.mn/n/3po4), written after the August 2026 minimum-wage decision, says the 2027 relief will be computed at **MNT 1,000,000**.
  - Seeded as 1,000,000, `unverified`, low, with the alternative in `value_text`. Confirm against the text of PIT Law art. 21.1.
- The monthly PIT credit table (20,000 down to 0) could not be re-confirmed, and its survival in 2027 is unknown.

**(g) Minimum wage.**
- **MNT 792,000** a month (4,714 an hour) from **2025-04-01**, under Tripartite Committee resolution No. 3 of 2024-10-07. This confirms the 2025-04-01 date in the earlier note; the fact-checker's recollection of 2025-01-01 was wrong.
- **MNT 1,000,000** a month from **2027-01-01**, decided on 2026-08-17 ([MLSP](https://mlsp.gov.mn/content/detail/4164), [mnb.mn](https://www.mnb.mn/i/350805), [ikon](https://ikon.mn/n/3po4)). Verified, high.

**(h) Ulaanbaatar city tax.**
- The rate is **2%**. The UB Citizens' Representatives Khural sets it within a statutory range of **0–2%**.
- It applies to retail alcohol and tobacco and to bar, restaurant, **hotel and resort** services. Hotels and resorts are therefore at 2%, not 1%.
- The Khural resolution of 2023-12-05 (in force 2024-01-01) also lists **car washes and auto-repair shops**. Only one source says so ([Qazinform](https://qazinform.com/news/mongolia-adopts-2024-budget-for-capital-city-3e58cf)).
- The base is the net price excluding VAT (PosAPI sample). The rate is verified, medium. The return form and filing frequency are still unknown.

**(i) Tax depreciation lives** (straight-line, CIT Law in force 2020-01-01).

| Asset class | Life for tax |
|---|---|
| Buildings | 25 years |
| Buildings of mineral and petroleum licence holders | 40 years |
| Machinery, equipment and vehicles | 10 years |
| Computers and software | 2 years |
| Other non-current assets | 10 years |
| New buildings outside central UB (optional, from 2023-01-01) | 15 years |

Sources: [PwC – Deductions](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/mongolia/corporate/deductions) and the KPMG 2023 changes PDF. An MTA PDF that gives 40/10/10 years is a copy of the **pre-2020** law. Verified, medium.

**(j) e-balance.**
- Annual financial statements are filed in e-balance between **1 January and 10 February** of the following year (verified, high). The MoF has granted ad-hoc extensions, for example to 14 February after a system overload.
- URL: `https://e-balance.mof.gov.mn/` (`unverified`, medium; the MoF how-to pages name the system, but the host was not reachable).
- The 20 July half-year filing applies to full-IFRS entities. Whether IFRS-for-SMEs entities are exempt is still `unverified`.
- No import file or API was found.

**(k) Approved accounting software rule (MoF Order 47 of 2018).**
- Confirmed:
  - Art. 17.1.11 of the Law on Accounting requires the MoF to publish a list of approved accounting software ([MoF list](https://mof.gov.mn/article/entry/12-20)).
  - MoF Order 47 of 2018, "requirements and guidelines for accounting software and establishment of a working group", sets the requirements ([MoF](https://mof.gov.mn/article/entry/2018-47)). A companion evaluation procedure exists.
  - MonICPA (ММНБИ) monitors approved software and assesses compliance.
- The requirements are compliance with the law, IFRS and IPSAS, and that transactions cannot be hidden, concealed or embezzled.
- Not obtained: the full checklist, the cost and the timeline of approval. This stays the top go-to-market blocker.
- The 2018-03-05 order date comes from one extract. The row is verified for the rule, medium.

**Also resolved:**
- **eBarimt:** receipts must reach the unified system within **72 hours** (verified, high).
- **Fine for not issuing a receipt:** **2% of the previous reporting month's revenue** under Law on Infringements art. 11.19 (verified, medium). When that revenue cannot be determined, a legal entity is fined 15,000 units (`unverified`).
- **Retention:** accounting records are kept for **at least 10 years**, unless the Law on Archives sets a longer period (verified, medium).
- **Class threshold:** under MoF Order A/25 of 2025-02-06, sales of **MNT 2.5 bn** or more move an entity to full IFRS (`unverified`, medium: only one underlying extract; the asset and headcount criteria were not found).

1. The PIT 0% band ceiling in 2027: a fixed MNT 792,000, or the minimum wage (MNT 1,000,000)? See PIT Law art. 21.1 as amended on 2026-06-26.
2. The start date of the simplified VAT regime (2027-01-01 or 2027-07-01), its eligibility rules, its due day and whether its base is VAT-exclusive.
3. Whether the 2027 simplified 1% regime at MNT 400 M replaces the 300 M CIT 1% rate (art. 20.2.7), the 50 M simplified return, or both.
4. The 2027 annual deadline (5 March) and quarterly payment deadline (last day of the following month): when do they first apply, and does the quarterly *filing* day also move?
5. The 2027 split of the employer accident and unemployment rates (0.3/1.2/2.2% and 0.6%).
6. The social-insurance report day (the 5th) and the PIT remittance day (the 10th): each has only one secondary source.
7. The monthly PIT credit table: does it survive in 2027?
8. eBarimt late-submission fines (500 and 5,000 units), the 15,000-unit fallback, and the fine-unit value of MNT 1,000.
9. The e-balance URL, Order A/205/2024, the full Order A/25 criteria, and the half-year filing scope.
10. Loss carry-forward (4 years, 50% cap), the CIT advance-payment day (25th), and the tax limitation period (4 years): each has a single source.

## Regenerating

Both the table above and the SQL seed are generated from one data script that was kept in the session scratchpad. When a value changes, edit both files consistently, then reload the seed. Use a throwaway database: running the file on an existing database first deletes every row for the parameter codes it seeds.

```
psql -h /var/lib/postgresql -p 55432 -U erp -d postgres -v ON_ERROR_STOP=1 -c "create database legalcheck"
psql -h /var/lib/postgresql -p 55432 -U erp -d legalcheck -v ON_ERROR_STOP=1 -f docs/features/mn-micro-erp/db/seed/legal_parameters.sql
psql -h /var/lib/postgresql -p 55432 -U erp -d postgres -v ON_ERROR_STOP=1 -c "drop database legalcheck"
```
